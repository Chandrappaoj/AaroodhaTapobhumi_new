Spiritual Programs

The Ashrama offers a rich calendar of spiritual programs designed to nourish the soul and deepen one's connection with the Divine.

Daily morning and evening Aarti ceremonies
Weekly Satsang with bhajans and spiritual discourses
Monthly Shiva Abhishekam and special pujas
Guided meditation sessions
Vedic chanting classes
Festival celebrations (Shivaratri, Navaratri, etc.)
