Annadanam
The Sacred Gift of Food

"Annadanam Maha Danam" - The gift of food is the greatest gift. Following this sacred principle, the Ashrama provides free meals to thousands of devotees, pilgrims, and needy individuals every day.

The Annadanam program operates 365 days a year, serving nutritious vegetarian meals prepared with love and devotion. No one who comes to the Ashrama leaves hungry.

This sacred service is made possible by the generous contributions of devotees and the dedicated volunteers who cook and serve with a spirit of worship.
